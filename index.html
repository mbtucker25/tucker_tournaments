Tucker Tournaments was created to carry on the spirit of David Tucker—a man whose kindness, strength, and quiet generosity touched everyone he met. Helping others wasn’t a mission he set out on; it was simply part of who he was.

David coached kids at every level, from youth leagues to high school teams, and led a Special Olympics group through his workplace. He didn’t do it for recognition. He did it because that’s who he was—someone who showed up, gave back, and lifted people up without needing a reason.

Even as he faced lung cancer, David kept his focus on others, especially the kids he mentored. That selflessness never left him. He continued to encourage, support, and inspire—just as he always had.

Tucker Tournaments was created in that same spirit. Through events like the David Tucker Memorial Golf Classic, we raise funds to support children facing serious health challenges. The money goes straight to families—helping cover medical bills, fulfill special wishes, and give kids a reason to smile in the middle of something hard.

It’s not just about honoring David. It’s about doing what he did—quietly, consistently, and with heart.

About This Year’s Athlete

Luke Turpin

Luke, 8 years old, was diagnosed with T cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia on October 24, 2024. He seemed healthy at first, but swollen lymph nodes prompted his pediatrician to order blood tests. The results came back fast and devastating. Luke had leukemia and was rushed to Riley Hospital for immediate treatment.

Further testing revealed a mass in his chest pressing against his airway and aorta. He was quickly moved to the Pediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) for specialized care. The situation was critical, and the medical team responded with urgency.

Luke’s treatment has been grueling. Chemotherapy caused him to lose 17% of his body weight, leaving him weak and struggling to recover. Things worsened when an undiagnosed infection led to endocarditis, forcing a six-week course of antibiotics delivered through his port. So far, he’s spent about two and a half months in the hospital which is an intense stretch that shows just how serious his condition has been.
