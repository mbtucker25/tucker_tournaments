About This Year’s Athlete

Luke Turpin

Luke, 8 years old, was diagnosed with T cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia on October 24, 2024. He seemed healthy at first, but swollen lymph nodes prompted his pediatrician to order blood tests. The results came back fast and devastating. Luke had leukemia and was rushed to Riley Hospital for immediate treatment.

Further testing revealed a mass in his chest pressing against his airway and aorta. He was quickly moved to the Pediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) for specialized care. The situation was critical, and the medical team responded with urgency.

Luke’s treatment has been grueling. Chemotherapy caused him to lose 17% of his body weight, leaving him weak and struggling to recover. Things worsened when an undiagnosed infection led to endocarditis, forcing a six-week course of antibiotics delivered through his port. So far, he’s spent about two and a half months in the hospital which is an intense stretch that shows just how serious his condition has been.

Event Details

The David Tucker Memorial Golf Classic celebrates the life and legacy of David Tucker, a man who stood tall not just in height at six feet eleven inches, but in character, compassion, and dedication to others. David was a Brownsburg High School graduate, a gifted athlete, and a four-year varsity basketball player. He was also a devoted husband, father, brother, and friend who faced lung cancer with remarkable strength and courage until his passing in 2021.

David's impact on others is undeniable. His smile, warmth, and generosity left a lasting impression on everyone who knew him. He gave his energy to coaching kids at every level—from youth leagues to high school—and led a Special Olympics team through his employer. His lessons reached far beyond sports. He showed young people how to grow into themselves, give back to others, and appreciate each day. Many of those he mentored are now pursuing their own goals, shaped by his encouragement and belief in them.

Helping others was always at the heart of David’s life, especially children and families across Indiana. Even while fighting cancer, he stayed focused on the kids he coached and inspired. His selflessness never faded.

Tucker Tournaments was created to honor that spirit and carry forward David’s mission. The annual Golf Classic is more than a tribute—it is a way to continue his work by supporting children facing serious health challenges. Proceeds from the tournament go directly to kids battling cancer or recovering from treatment. The funds help ease the burden of medical bills or bring a special wish to life. In doing so, the event brings hope to families, just as David did every day of his life.

Join us for a great day on the course and help make a lasting difference.
